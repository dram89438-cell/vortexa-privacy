Child Safety Standards — Vortexa

App: Vortexa (Android, package com.ganesh.vortexa) · Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Contact: dram89438@gmail.com

Vortexa has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We do not allow it anywhere in the app, and we remove it, report it and act against the accounts involved.

1. Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)

CSAE includes any content or behaviour that sexually abuses, exploits or endangers a child (a person under 18). The following is strictly prohibited on Vortexa, in videos, photos, captions, comments, profiles, usernames or any other part of the app:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including images, videos or links to such material.
  • Sexualised content involving minors, including content that presents a child in a sexual way.
  • Grooming, or any attempt to contact, befriend or influence a child for sexual purposes.
  • Sextortion, or threatening or pressuring a child to produce or share sexual content.
  • Trafficking of children, or offers to buy, sell or trade sexual content involving children.
  • Content that promotes, praises or gives instructions for any of the above.

Vortexa is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

2. In-app reporting and moderation

  • How to report: use the Report option in the app and choose Child safety concern (or the reason that fits best). You can add the details that help us review it.
  • Review: reports are reviewed by our team. We use automated tools to help us sort reports, and reports about child safety are treated as the highest priority for human review.
  • Immediate action: content reported as possible CSAE may be hidden from public view straight away while it is reviewed.
  • Other ways to report: you can also email us at dram89438@gmail.com. Please do not attach or forward the material itself. Tell us where to find it in the app (for example the account name or the post).

3. Law enforcement and regional authority cooperation

  • When we find apparent CSAM, we report it to the relevant authorities as required by the laws that apply to us. This includes the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline where applicable, and the Indian authorities, such as through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and the police.
  • We respond to valid legal requests from law enforcement and keep relevant information when the law requires us to.
  • We follow the child protection laws that apply to our service, including India’s Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 and the Information Technology Act.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact the police (112 in India) or CHILDLINE (1098) right away.

4. Account enforcement and removal

  • We remove content that breaks these standards.
  • We suspend or permanently remove accounts that post, share, request or promote CSAE. We do not give a warning first for serious violations.
  • We may use information about removed accounts to help prevent repeat abuse, as allowed by law.
  • If you believe we made a mistake, you can contact us at the email below and a person will look at it again.
