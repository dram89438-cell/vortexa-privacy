Vortexa Privacy Policy

App: Vortexa (Android, package com.ganesh.vortexa) · Developer contact: dram89438@gmail.com · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Englishहिन्दी

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Vortexa app (“Vortexa”, “we”, “us”) collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using Vortexa you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and profile: email address, password (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password in readable form), display name, bio and a unique user ID.
  • Content you create: posts (photos, videos, titles, captions and tags), likes, comments, follows and saved posts. Your posts and public profile can be seen by other users.
  • Photos and videos: the app accesses only the files you choose to upload. Uploaded media is stored in Firebase Storage.
  • Notifications and activity: in-app notifications about activity on your account.
  • Safety, reports and support: reports you submit, copyright claims you file (your name, email, a description of your work and a link to it), support tickets, and your conversations with the in-app AI assistant.
  • Creator earnings and payouts (only if you use these features): ad-earnings records, payout requests, payout method details (a UPI ID or bank details, stored encrypted and shown only to authorised administrators when a payment is processed), identity-verification status, and fraud-prevention signals such as account age and request patterns.
  • Advertising data: Vortexa shows ads using Google AdMob. Google may collect and use device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. We also record ad impressions (which account saw an ad and its estimated value) to calculate creator earnings and detect fraud.
  • Technical data: the Google services used by the app (Firebase and AdMob) may automatically process technical information such as IP address, device model, operating-system version and app or installation identifiers, for security and to operate the service. The app also stores small preferences on your device.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the app: accounts, feed, search, notifications and your profile.
  • To store and display the content you post.
  • To moderate content, enforce our rules, and handle reports, copyright claims and support requests.
  • To calculate and pay creator earnings, verify identity, and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To show ads.
  • To keep the app secure, fix problems and improve it.
  • To comply with the law.

3. Service providers we use

  • Firebase / Google Cloud (Google LLC): Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage and Cloud Functions store your account data and content and run our backend.
  • Google AdMob: serves ads in the app.
  • Google Gemini API: powers our AI features. Text you send to the in-app AI assistant, together with limited information about your own account (for example your payout status and recent requests), and the content of reports, copyright claims and support tickets, may be sent to Google’s Gemini API so that an AI model can draft answers, summarise cases and suggest a priority. Automated checks may temporarily hide reported content until a person reviews it. AI output is used to help our review. If a decision affects you significantly, you can contact us and ask for a person to look at it.

4. How we share information

  • Your public profile, posts and comments are visible to other users.
  • We share data with the service providers above only as needed to run Vortexa.
  • We may disclose information when required by law, or to protect users, prevent fraud or abuse, or enforce our rules.
  • If the app is transferred to another owner, your information may be transferred with it, subject to this policy.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active.

Delete your account in the app: open your Profile, tap the menu, choose Delete account and enter your password. This deletes your posts, your profile and your login. An automated cleanup also removes related data (such as followers, saved items and notifications) and the files you uploaded.

If you cannot use the app: email dram89438@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject “Delete my Vortexa account”. We may ask you to verify your identity before we act.

What may be kept after deletion: records related to earnings and payouts (wallet, payout and ledger records) are kept to meet financial record-keeping obligations. Some records of reports, copyright claims, support requests and security or audit logs may also be kept where needed for safety, fraud prevention or legal reasons. Backups and caches can take some time to clear. We cannot delete copies that other people have saved or shared.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Payout details are stored encrypted, and administrator access is limited and logged. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can edit your profile and delete your posts or your account in the app.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • You can contact us to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Depending on where you live (including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have additional rights.

8. Children

Vortexa is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Where data is processed

Your data is processed on Google’s servers, which may be located in India or in other countries.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page with a new “Last updated” date. If you keep using Vortexa after a change, you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact us

Questions or requests about privacy: dram89438@gmail.com.
